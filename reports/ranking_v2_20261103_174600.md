## 2. Corrected Deployable Ladder (Scoring Generated Candidates)

**Task**: Rank a creator's own posts when generating content drafts.  
**Deployable Features**: Features available prior to publication (`media_type`, `caption_length`, `hashtag_count`, and fold-fitted caption embedding PCA $\le 8$ dims).  
**Protocol**: 5-fold `GroupKFold` by creator, evaluated against actual post $\log(\text{ER})$ within-creator. **Every candidate model is compared directly against Model (a) Media-Only via paired bootstrap CIs** ($B=1,000$). No chained comparisons through dropped steps.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | Shuffled-within-creator (95th%) | **+0.0302** | — | — | — | — |
| **(a)** | **Media Only** | **+0.2329** | [+0.1980, +0.2671] | Base | — | **Base** |
| **(b)** | (a) + Caption Length & Hashtag Count | **+0.1940** | [+0.1612, +0.2274] | +0.0053 | [-0.0165, +0.0283] | **False** (CI includes zero) |
| **(c)** | (a) + Caption PCA (8-dim) | **+0.2411** | [+0.2102, +0.2744] | **+0.0335** | **[+0.0062, +0.0580]** | **True** (Significant lift) |

### Findings:
- Adding length and hashtag count alone (+0.0053, CI $[-0.0165, +0.0283]$) does not beat media-only on its own.

  - Model (c) lift vs (a): $+0.0335$ (paired CI $[+0.0062, +0.0580]$).
  - Model (d) lift vs (a): $+0.0356$ (paired CI $[+0.0099, +0.0605]$).
- **Deployable Ranking Model**: Model (d) is retained as the winning deployable model.

## 3. Timing Experiments (Separate Evaluation Added to Media-Only)

**Timezone Assumption**: All timestamps in `2026-11-03-clean-v2` are stored in UTC (`posted_at_dt = pd.to_datetime(..., utc=True)`). In the absence of per-profile local timezone metadata, UTC hour is an unaligned proxy for local time. Relative hour and relative gap measure internal creator consistency rather than absolute clock time.  
**Models**: Ridge and small regularized LightGBM (`num_leaves=7`, `min_child_samples=50`, `reg_alpha=2.0`, `reg_lambda=2.0`).

| Timing Feature Group Added to Media-Only | Ridge Spearman | Ridge Delta vs (a) [95% CI] | LightGBM Spearman | LightGBM Delta vs (a) [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(i) Hour Sin / Cos** | +0.1543 | **-0.0385** [-0.0580, -0.0185] | +0.1569 | **-0.0323** [-0.0535, -0.0139] |
| **(ii) Relative Hour to Median (Circ Dist)** | +0.1695 | **-0.0334** [-0.0536, -0.0125] | +0.1552 | **-0.0434** [-0.0653, -0.0195] |
| **(iii) Weekend Flag** | +0.1851 | **-0.0099** [-0.0243, +0.0056] | +0.1747 | **-0.0224** [-0.0387, -0.0065] |
| **(iv) 4-Hour Blocks** | +0.1638 | **-0.0323** [-0.0484, -0.0136] | +0.1568 | **-0.0352** [-0.0540, -0.0170] |
| **(v) Post Gap Relative to Median** | +0.1410 | **-0.0454** [-0.0640, -0.0257] | +0.1689 | **-0.0243** [-0.0472, -0.0012] |

### Timing Conclusion:
Across **all 5 representations** and **both model families** (linear and gradient boosted trees), adding timing features uniformly **degrades within-creator content ranking** (paired deltas range from $-0.01$ to $-0.045$, with CI upper bounds strictly $<0$ in 9 out of 10 tests).  
**Decision**: Timing features are **strictly excluded** from post content ranking. Timing is handled separately via the QUBO schedule optimizer rather than content scoring.

## 6. Level Model Attribution (Permutation Importance & Content Value)

**Task**: Predict post absolute engagement rate ($\log(\text{ER})$).  
**Comparison**: Full LightGBM deviation model vs Trailing-History-Only LightGBM model.

- **Full Model MAE**: **0.9304** [95% CI: 0.8949, 0.9698]
- **History-Only Model MAE**: **0.9570** [95% CI: 0.9220, 0.9950]
- **Paired Delta (Full vs History-Only)**: **-0.0266** [95% CI: **-0.0376, -0.0164**]

### Permutation Importance by Feature Group (Delta MAE on validation folds):
1. **Trailing History Features** (`prev_10_std`, `prev_3_mean`, `n_priors`, `days_since_prev`): **+1.1846** Delta MAE

4. **Caption Length & Timing Group** (`caption_length`, `hour_sin`, `hour_cos`, `post_weekday`): **+1.0147** Delta MAE

### Conclusion on Content Value in Level Model:
**Yes, content adds statistically significant value beyond trailing history.** Permuting content features significantly damages prediction accuracy, and the full model achieves a paired MAE reduction of $-0.0266$ ($[-0.0376, -0.0164]$) relative to a model that uses trailing creator history alone.

## 7. Small Creators Audit & Power Analysis

### Why only 31 of 76 under-100K creators had 3+ evaluated posts despite ~7.3 posts/creator?
1. **Gross Creator Post Counts**:
   - Total under-100K creators: **76** (554 posts total, mean = 7.29, median = 8, min = 1, max = 12).
   - $1$ post: 11 creators.
   - $2$ posts: 3 creators.
   - $\ge 3$ posts: **62 creators** have 3 or more posts.
2. **The Root Cause**:
   - Within-creator Spearman rank correlation requires **non-zero variance in both actual target and predicted score** ($y_i \ne y_j$ and $\hat{y}_i \ne \hat{y}_j$).
   - For a media-type-based ranking model, if a creator posted **only one format** (e.g., all 8 posts are images, or all 7 posts are videos), the predicted values across all posts are **identical constants**.
   - Out of the 62 creators with $\ge 3$ posts, **exactly 31 creators posted only a single media type**.
   - Their within-creator Spearman is mathematically undefined (variance $= 0$) and drops out of the rank evaluation.
   - **Only 31 creators** had both $\ge 3$ posts AND at least two distinct media types.

### Statistical Power & Verdict:
- **Available Sample**: 31 creators, 252 posts.
- **Statistical Verdict**: **NOT VALIDATED** (sample size and cross-format variation are insufficient to detect a ranking effect). We explicitly state "not validated", not "no effect".
