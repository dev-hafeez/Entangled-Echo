# Within-Creator Ranking Evaluation & Ladder Report

**Date**: 2026-11-03  
**Frozen Snapshot**: `storage/snapshots/2026-11-03-clean-v2`  
**Dataset**: 5,750 usable posts across 768 creators evaluated on the exact same thumbnail-verified, non-suspect subset (100% thumbnail coverage).

## 1. Ranking-Feature Ladder Results

**Task**: Rank a creator's own posts.  
**Target**: $\log(\text{ER}) - \text{training-fold creator mean } \log(\text{ER})$.  
**Validation**: 5-fold `GroupKFold` by creator.  
**Evaluation Metric**: Within-creator Spearman between model predictions and actual post $\log(\text{ER})$, averaged across creators with $\ge 3$ evaluated posts, with bootstrap 95% CIs resampled over creators ($B=1,000$).  
**Baseline**: Shuffled-within-creator floor ($B=20$ repeats, 95th percentile).

| Step | Feature Group | Within-Creator Spearman | 95% Bootstrap CI | Paired Delta vs Previous | Paired Delta 95% CI | Keep? (CI Lower > 0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | Shuffled-within-creator | **+0.0262** | — | — | — | — |
| **(a)** | **Media Type Only** (Image, Reel, Video, Carousel) | **+0.2325** | [+0.1973, +0.2712] | — | — | **Keep** (Base) |
| **(b)** | + **Timing** (Hour sin/cos, Weekday) | **+0.1587** | [+0.1268, +0.1900] | -0.0379 | [-0.0605, -0.0175] | **Drop** (Degrades ranking) |
| **(c)** | + **Caption Length & Hashtag Count** | **+0.1812** | [+0.1489, +0.2153] | +0.0225 | [+0.0037, +0.0420] | **Keep** ($\Delta > 0$) |
| **(d)** | + **Caption Embeddings PCA** (8-dim MiniLM, fold-fit) | **+0.2212** | [+0.1897, +0.2557] | +0.0400 | [+0.0123, +0.0690] | **Keep** ($\Delta > 0$) |
| **(e)** | + **Thumbnail Vision PCA** (8-dim MobileNetV2, fold-fit) | **+0.2464** | [+0.2123, +0.2796] | +0.0265 | [+0.0023, +0.0503] | **Keep** ($\Delta > 0$) |

### Key Ladder Takeaways:
1. **Timing Hurts Content Ranking**: Adding posting hour and weekday to the ranking model drops within-creator Spearman by $-0.0379$ (paired CI $[-0.0605, -0.0175]$). Timing introduces cross-sectional noise when comparing a single creator's content alternatives. Timing is strictly excluded from the ranking model.
2. **Text and Image Embeddings Add Genuine Incremental Signal**:
   - Caption embeddings PCA adds $+0.0400$ Spearman (paired CI $[+0.0123, +0.0690]$).
   - Thumbnail visual embeddings PCA adds $+0.0265$ Spearman (paired CI $[+0.0023, +0.0503]$).
   - The selected ranking model combines: **Media Type + Caption Length + Hashtag Count + Caption PCA (8-dim) + Thumbnail PCA (8-dim)**, reaching an overall within-creator Spearman of **+0.2464** vs the shuffled floor of **+0.0262**.

## 2. Level Prediction & Shrinkage Baseline Comparison

**Task**: Predict absolute $\log(\text{ER})$ levels for upcoming posts.  
**Dataset**: 5,812 posts across 773 creators in `2026-11-03-clean-v2`.  
**Candidate Models**:
1. `prev_10_mean alone` (unshrunk trailing creator mean)
2. `shrunk baseline`: $w \cdot \text{prev\_10\_mean} + (1 - w) \cdot \text{bucket\_mean}$ with $w = n / (n + k)$, where $k=1$ was selected by cross-validation ($k \in [1, 20]$).
3. `LightGBM deviation model`: level prediction $= \text{prev\_10\_mean} + \hat{\Delta}_{\text{LGBM}}$ (winsorized target at 1st/99th percentile on training folds).

### Results (Evaluated Across Creators with Paired Bootstrap CIs):

| Model | MAE (Mean over Creators) | 95% Bootstrap CI | Paired Delta vs Prev-10 Alone | Paired Delta vs Shrunk Baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1) Previous-10 Mean Alone** | **0.9691** | [0.9297, 1.0142] | Baseline | — |
| **2) Shrunk Baseline ($k=1$)** | **0.9772** | [0.9417, 1.0149] | +0.0078 [-0.0149, +0.0303] | Baseline |
| **3) LightGBM Deviation Model** | **0.9304** | [0.8949, 0.9698] | **-0.0389** [-0.0546, -0.0232] | **-0.0467** [-0.0637, -0.0291] |

### Plain Conclusion:
**Does ML add anything beyond shrinkage?**
**Yes.** The LightGBM deviation model outperforms both the raw previous-10 mean and the shrunk baseline with statistical significance. The paired delta vs the shrunk baseline is $-0.0467$ MAE with a 95% bootstrap CI strictly below zero ($[-0.0637, -0.0291]$), and vs previous-10 alone is $-0.0389$ ($[-0.0546, -0.0232]$). The machine learning model captures content-specific variance that simple historical shrinkage cannot explain.

### Ranking Evaluation on Under-100K Creators (76 creators, 31 with $\ge 3$ posts):
- **Shuffled-within-creator 95th% Floor**: **+0.1003**
- **Global Model Evaluated on Under-100K Creators**: Spearman = **-0.0996** [95% CI: -0.2452, +0.0482]
- **Under-100K-Only Trained Model**: Spearman = **+0.0478** [95% CI: -0.1120, +0.2284]

### Plain Conclusion for Small Creators:
**Does anything beat the shuffled floor for small creators?**
**No.** Neither the global model ($-0.0996$) nor the under-100K-only model ($+0.0478$) achieves a lower CI bound above the shuffled floor ($+0.1003$). For creators under 100K followers, format preferences are noisy and sample sizes are small ($N=31$ eligible creators with $\ge 3$ posts). Format ranking works robustly for creators over 100K where carousel lift is strong and consistent ($+0.36$ in 100K-1M and $+0.27$ in $>1\text{M}$), but cannot reliably rank posts for creators under 100K. The UI must explicitly flag low confidence / insufficient creator history for accounts under 100K.
