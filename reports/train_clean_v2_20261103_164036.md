- **Shuffled Order Trailing Mean Spearman (20 repeats)**: **`-0.3152`** (Range: `[-0.3511, -0.2759]`).
- **Diagnosis**: The negative rank correlation of the trailing mean is an artifact of small-sample cumulative windowing ($ar{y}_{<t}$ vs $y_t$). Because each creator has only 8–12 posts, when random post orders are shuffled, the correlation is even *more* negative (-0.3152). As the window expands, the sample variance of the mean contracts, causing higher index realizations to consistently outrank earlier small-sample anchors.
